How to Return an Item

  1. 1
    Contact us within 14 days

    Message us on WhatsApp at +94 71 902 5444 or email info@ghanimenterprises.lk with your order number and the reason for return.

  2. 2
    Get approval

    We will confirm eligibility and provide you with the return address within 24 hours.

  3. 3
    Ship the item back

    Pack the item securely in its original packaging and send it via any courier of your choice. Return shipping cost is at your expense.

  4. 4
    Receive exchange or store credit

    Once we receive and inspect the item (1–2 business days), we will process your exchange or issue store credit within 2 business days.

Return Conditions

✅ Eligible for return

  • Item received in damaged or defective condition
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Item in original, unused condition with original packaging
  • Return requested within 14 days of delivery

❌ Not eligible for return

  • Items that have been used or washed
  • Items missing original packaging or tags
  • Returns requested after 14 days of delivery
  • Items damaged after delivery due to misuse
  • Perishable or hygiene-sensitive products

Refund Method

We offer exchange for an item of equal value or store credit valid for 12 months on ghanimenterprises.lk. We do not offer cash refunds at this time.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, please send us a photo via WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. In this case we will arrange a replacement or store credit at no additional cost to you.
